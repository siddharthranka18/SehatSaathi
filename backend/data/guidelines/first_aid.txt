## Minor Cuts and Burns
For minor cuts: clean with running water, apply antiseptic, cover with a clean bandage. For minor burns (small, no blistering): cool under running water for 10 minutes, do not apply ice directly, cover loosely. Seek medical attention if the wound is deep, won't stop bleeding, or the burn covers a large area or blisters significantly.

## Fainting
If someone faints briefly and regains consciousness quickly: lay them flat, elevate legs slightly, ensure fresh air, loosen tight clothing. If they do not regain consciousness within a minute, or fainting repeats, this is critical and needs urgent medical attention.

## Choking
If a person is choking but can still cough or speak: encourage continued coughing. If they cannot speak, cough, or breathe, this is a critical emergency requiring immediate first aid and urgent medical help.
