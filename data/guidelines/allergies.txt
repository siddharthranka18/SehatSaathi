Allergies - General Guidance

Common presentation:
- Itchy eyes, sneezing, runny or blocked nose
- Skin hives (urticaria), redness, or localized swelling
- Mild respiratory symptoms (nasal congestion) without severe breathing difficulty

When to seek urgent care:
- Difficulty breathing, wheeze, throat tightness, or facial swelling
- Signs of anaphylaxis (rapid swelling, difficulty speaking, fainting)

Home care:
- Remove exposure to suspected allergen if possible
- Use oral antihistamines for itch and hives (if appropriate and not contraindicated)
- Seek medical assessment if symptoms worsen or do not improve

Note: This is general information, not a diagnosis. For severe allergic reactions, call emergency services immediately.
