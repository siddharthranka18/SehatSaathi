Fungal Infection - General Guidance

Common presentation:
- Localized itching, redness, scaling, or rash (e.g., ring-shaped for ringworm)
- Nail changes: thickening, discoloration
- Genital yeast infections: itching, discharge

When to seek medical care:
- Widespread skin involvement, severe pain, fever, or signs of bacterial superinfection
- Nail or scalp infections not improving with OTC measures

Home care:
- Keep affected area clean and dry
- Use topical antifungal creams for skin infections as directed on packaging
- Seek medical advice for recurrent or persistent infections

Note: This information is general guidance and not a clinical diagnosis. See a healthcare professional for persistent or severe symptoms.
